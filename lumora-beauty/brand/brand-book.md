## 1. The story

### The hour of light

Golden hour is the one light that flatters everyone. The low sun is warm, soft and directional. It fills in
texture, warms every undertone and makes skin look lit from within, on fair and deep skin alike. It is also the
moment the day slows down.

Lumora is built on that observation, and the name already holds it: **lumen + ora, the hour of light.** The
brand promises both halves of golden hour.

**The look.** Skin that looks warm-lit, even and soft-focus. The glow comes from skincare (vitamin C,
carotenoid-rich oils, barrier care and light-diffusing textures), never from mica, glitter or self-tan. The glow
formulas carry no tint; Apricity's sheer color comes from iron oxides.

**The feeling.** Ten slow minutes. Golden hour comes twice a day, after sunrise and before sunset, so the ritual
does too. The evening half carries on beyond the face, into the body (Gloaming oil) and a warm cup (Slow Hour
golden milk). That is the Health in Health & Beauty, with no health claims attached.

### Why it belongs to Lumora

The idea comes from the name itself. Three devices express it, and no one else can claim all three:

1. **The logo.** The "o" in the wordmark is a sun touching the horizon.
2. **The shelf.** The six products step through a sunset, from sand to dusk, and each label's sun mark sinks one
   notch.
3. **The homepage.** Color schemes warm as you scroll and reach dusk at the footer.

## 3. Logo

### The wordmark

Lowercase **lumora**, set in Fraunces SuperSoft at weight 380 and optical size 144, tracked -1%, then outlined
and redrawn by hand. The wordmark is artwork: it is never typed as live text.

### The setting-sun "o"

- The "o" is cut by a horizontal gap, the horizon, at 66.5 to 71% of the glyph box, about one third up its
  height.
- The sliver below the horizon turns **Terracotta** on light grounds and **Honey** on Dusk, like a sun touching
  the horizon.
- The gap is 4.5% of the cap height, so it survives at 24 px wide. Below 80 px wide, use the one-color version
  with the gap widened to 7%.
- In one-color use (labels, foil, embossing) the cut stays and the color doesn't change.

### The low sun (monogram)

A solid disc cut by the same horizon gap. It is the favicon, app tile, map pin, sun dot, wax-seal box sticker,
label sun mark and lid-top decal.

| Colorway | Use |
|---|---|
| Honey on Dusk | Primary tile, favicon |
| The golden-hour gradient on Linen | Feature moments, the proof section |
| Linen on Terracotta | Stickers, accents on the accent color |

### Descriptor lockup

The wordmark, a hairline, then **BEAUTY** in Figtree 600 at +28% tracking. The line *The hour of light* in
Fraunces italic can sit beneath it.

### Clear space and minimum size

| | |
|---|---|
| **Clear space** | One "o" width on every side |
| **Minimum size** | Wordmark 72 px or 18 mm wide; monogram 16 px |

### Please don't

- Fill the wordmark with a gradient. The one exception is the giant sign-off in the footer.
- Add outlines or shadows.
- Set it in uppercase.
- Re-type the logo in live text without the cut "o".

### Heading rules

- **One italic accent word per heading, always the last word**, as in Late light, *bottled.* It takes the
  scheme's accent color: Terracotta on Travertine and Linen, Clay on Sand and the Honey and Apricot washes, Dusk
  on Rose wash, and Honey on Terracotta, Clay and Dusk.
- **Six words or fewer** per headline, in sentence case. Never all caps.
- **Eyebrows of two to four words**, on story sections only. Product rails get a bare heading.
- **One section-header pattern everywhere**, the product page included: eyebrow, H2, lead, then a right-aligned
  "view all" link where there is one.

## 6. Voice

### Warm, unhurried, exact

Lumora sounds like a friend who notices the light: calm, sensory and precise. It speaks to you, uses "we" for
the brand, and names the ingredient, the percentage and the hour. It does not hype.

### Principles

- **Light and time are the shared vocabulary:** late light, low sun, the last hour, twice a day, by week four. One
  light word per section at most.
- **Contrast pairs carry the promise:** "Warm, never orange." "Lit, never glittery." One device per block.
- **Numbers beat adjectives,** and every statistic carries its footnote on the same screen.
- **A lexicon, used lightly.** Product names are words for light, and each product page opens with a one-line
  definition.
- **No exclamation marks and no emoji.** Sentence case, the Oxford comma and US spelling.

| We say | We never say |
|---|---|
| warm, lit, soft-focus, satin, plush, slow, late light, twice a day, looks, feels, helps | sun-kissed, bronzed, tan, after-sun, flawless, miracle, treats, repairs, heals, anti-aging, detox, clean, chemical-free |

### Sample lines

| Where | Line |
|---|---|
| Brand | Golden hour happens twice a day. So does the ritual. |
| Hero | Late light, bottled. |
| Product page | The color is the ingredient: sea buckthorn's own carotenoids. No dyes. |
| Lexicon | *gloaming (n.) the soft light after sunset.* Also: your shoulders, after this oil. |
| Ingredients | We leave out mica and glitter. The glow should be your skin's, not a coating. |
| Science | Scattered, skin looks flat. Returned, it looks lit. |
| Cart | You're $11 from free shipping. An Apricity would do it. |
| Newsletter | The Low Sun Letter. One note a fortnight, sent at your golden hour. |
| 404 | You've wandered past sunset. Back to the light. |

### Claims, kept cosmetic

A golden-hour brand has to be careful with the sun. These rules hold on every page, label and post:

- **No sun care.** The brand never implies sun protection, tanning or after-sun care. No beaches, swimwear or
  bronzed skin. Ritual graphics show *your daily SPF* as an unbranded morning step.
- **Appearance and feel only:** "brightens the look of dull skin", "evens the look of tone", "helps replenish the
  moisture barrier", "comforts dry-feeling skin". Never treat, cure, heal or repair.
- **Every number is footnoted.** The marker sits next to the figure, the method sits directly beneath the
  module, and the data is labeled as illustrative placeholder data.
- **Slow Hour is a food.** Its copy stays sensory, with no health, "beauty from within" or supplement claims.
- **Honest proof.** No certification marks or press logos we haven't earned, no "clinically proven", no "up to"
  presented as typical, and no fake urgency.

## 9. Art direction

### One light for renders and photographs

We render every product ourselves in Blender (Cycles), so each pack carries the real Lumora label and the six
read as one line. Skin, hands and textures come from Unsplash. One grade ties them together, and every image
passes the same test: **honey highlights, dusk shadows, nothing pure black or white.**

### Packshots

*For product cards, the product page's first image, the cart and the mega menu.*

- **Backdrop:** a seamless sweep in Sand `#E9D9C5`, matched exactly to the card tile so packs sit seamlessly in
  the grid.
- **Light:** a soft, warm key from the left at about 4,800 K, a warm rim on each side and a soft halo behind the
  glass.
- **Camera:** 90 mm lens at 6° elevation, the product filling 72% of the frame height, label square to camera.
  The same camera height and scale family for all six.
- **Output:** 4:5 at 2048 px on the long edge.
- **Hover image:** the same pack, camera and crop in low sun. On hover, the light changes.

### Low sun

*For hover images, the hero and the homepage scenes.*

- **Light:** a hard, raking, warm sun at 3,300 K from the left, 12° above the horizon, with olive-leaf shadows
  across the set.
- **Sets:** travertine plinths and steps, at most one glass sphere, an olive sprig, and plaster for evening scenes.
- **The signature shot:** amber glass throws its own color into its shadow, so every amber product has a glowing
  orange core inside its cast shadow. Caustics are kept for hero scenes.
- **Walls:** `#E7D2BC` in shade and `#F4C996` where the sun falls.

### Photography brief

- **Subjects:** skin in late sun at close and medium crops (cheekbones, jawlines, collarbones, shoulders, backs of
  necks, hands and wrists); hands holding a warm cup; leaf or window-light shadows on skin; textures such as
  apricot halves, sea buckthorn berries, honey and oil drops, linen in sun, terracotta and travertine.
- **Light:** low sun, side or back lit, warm (about 3,000 to 3,800 K), with hard-edged leaf shadows. Window light
  at golden hour is as welcome as outdoor light.
- **Casting:** Fitzpatrick I to VI in every set of four, ages 25 to 60, all genders. Real texture stays: pores,
  fine hairs, freckles, stretch marks and hyperpigmentation. Minimal makeup, eyes closed or in profile, candid
  rather than posed.
- **We avoid:** beaches, swimwear, sunbathing or anything that suggests sun care; glitter or shimmer; heavily
  retouched or filtered skin; teal-orange or cool grades; silhouettes without skin detail; visible brands or text;
  stock smiles at camera; anything that could pass for a customer before-and-after.
- **Crops:** 4:5 for portraits and cards, 3:4 for gallery tiles, 16:9 for bands only. Every photographer is
  credited.

## 10. Shape, motion and detail

### Shape

- **One radius:** 14 px for cards, tiles, images and drawers; fully rounded pills for buttons, inputs and chips.
- **Hairlines before shadows.** Shadows are soft, long and tinted with Dusk or Honey, never gray.
- **Spacing:** sections breathe at 64 to 120 px (feature sections 80 to 160 px). Content sits in a 1320 px
  container with 60 px gutters on desktop, on one left edge from header to footer.

### Buttons

- **Primary:** a Terracotta pill with Linen text, 52 px tall (48 on mobile). On hover it deepens to Clay, gains a
  soft 7 px Honey halo and lifts 1 px, and its arrow nudges 3 px. On dark sections it becomes a Linen or Honey pill
  with Dusk text.
- **Secondary:** the same pill as a 1 px outline.
- **Text link:** Figtree 600 in the accent color with a fine underline.
- **Focus:** a crisp contrast ring inside the same Honey halo, so keyboard focus is clear on every section.

### Icons

A custom set drawn in a 1.4 px line on a 32 px grid, with rounded caps and joins. Every icon sits on a short
horizon hairline. The set covers shipping, the guarantee (with a sun dot), every skin tone (three overlapping
circles), amber glass, vegan formula, golden hour, fragrance-free (a drop) and the how-to-use steps. Icons are
Dusk on light grounds and Linen on dark.

### Motifs

- **Sun marks.** An arc above a horizon line, with a sun dot that sinks from step 01 (high left) to step 06 (half
  set). They appear on labels, product cards, the product page eyebrow, the mega menu and the ritual timeline.
- **Leaf light.** The same leaf shadows that fall on our renders, laid over two web sections at most at 6%,
  drifting slowly.
- **The horizon edge.** A shallow convex arc, like the curve of the horizon. It appears exactly twice: under the
  hero and into the footer.
- **Glow.** One soft Honey-to-Apricot bloom, behind the hero products and the closing band only.
- **Sun-dot footnotes.** Footnote markers sit in small Honey discs.

### Motion

One easing curve, *slow sunset* (`cubic-bezier(.25,.8,.25,1)`), at two speeds: 200 ms for color and 600 ms for
light. Images zoom to 1.04 over 900 ms on hover; sections fade up 24 px as they arrive. Everything is switched
off for visitors who prefer reduced motion.
