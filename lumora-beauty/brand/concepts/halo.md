## 1. Big idea

**Light is the first ingredient.**

Most "glow" brands treat light as a mood. Lumora treats it as physics. Dry or uneven skin scatters light and reads dull. Smooth, hydrated, even skin sends light back in one direction and reads lit from within. So every Lumora formula has one optical job: **clear the surface, hold the water, even the tone, or seal it in.**

The packaging proves the thesis. We left out the actives that break down in light (vitamin C and retinoids), so the formulas don't need to hide in brown glass. They sit in frosted and opal glass with every percentage on the front. The one exception is honest: vitamin D3 *is* light-sensitive, so Sunroom wears amber.

The visual world follows: one disc of morning sun falls through an oculus-like aperture onto travertine, and a frosted bottle glows inside it, throwing a small golden caustic onto the stone. Everything is built from light behaving beautifully on glass and stone. The result is sculptural and calm, never sparkly.

**Rules**
- Specific beats superlative. Use numerals, percentages, minutes and kelvin. "Three drops, thirty seconds," never "luxurious texture."
- One light image per block at most (glint, halo, first light, window light). Never stack metaphors.
- Cosmetic claims only: "the look of," "skin looks," "helps," "feels." Put a footnote marker beside every number, with the method directly beneath.

## 6. Typography

| Role | Family | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Display** | **Bodoni Moda** (OFL, Google Fonts), weights 400/500 plus 400 italic, **fixed at optical size 28** | H1–H3, product names, the wordmark, step numerals (italic), pull quotes, the "lit word" (italic) |
| **Body/UI** | **Jost** (OFL; in Shopify's font library) 400/500 | Body, nav, buttons, eyebrows (tracked caps), prices (tabular), spec rows, captions |
| **Accent** | No third family. Bodoni Moda *Italic* is the accent voice. | Numerals ("No. 03"), lit words, testimonial quotes |

**Why this pair.** A Didone's thick-thin contrast is literally light and shadow on a carved edge: sculptural and clean-luxe. At its default display optical size (96) the hairlines vanish on 1x screens; at opsz 28 they hold. Jost is a geometric sans whose round "o" echoes the halo, and it is precise at 13–17 px. The pairing also avoids every reference face (Cormorant Garamond and Hanken Grotesk on Inzinnergy, Anton and Hanken on Convenz, Barlow Condensed and Source Sans on Coldbrook).

**Scale (desktop / mobile)**

| Token | Spec |
|---|---|
| H1 (hero) | Bodoni 400, `clamp(40px, 6.4vw, 92px)`, line-height .98, letter-spacing −1.2%, sentence case |
| H2 (section) | Bodoni 400, `clamp(32px, 3.6vw, 52px)`, lh 1.04, −1% |
| H3 / product title | Bodoni 400, 32 / 24 px, lh 1.1 |

| Light caption | Bodoni italic 16 px for the product line + Jost 500 10.5 px, +18% caps for "7:40 am · 4300 K · travertine" |

**Minimog setup**
- **Heading font: custom upload.** Bodoni Moda is not in Shopify's library. Generate static instances at opsz 28 from the variable font (`fonttools varLib.instancer "BodoniModa[opsz,wght].ttf" opsz=28 wght=400`, and the same for 500 and the italic), convert to WOFF2, upload to Content > Files and paste the URLs in Theme settings > Typography > Heading (custom font, one URL per weight). Add the italic `@font-face` (`font-family: M-Heading-Font; font-style: italic`) in the skin's Custom Liquid, because the theme field only declares normal styles. This avoids the faux italics the research found on Inzinnergy.
- **Alternative:** upload the variable WOFF2 once, declare `font-weight: 400 700` in the skin `@font-face`, and set `font-variation-settings: "opsz" 28` on `h1, h2, .h1, .h2`.
- **Body font: Jost from the library** works. For a true 500 weight (buttons and eyebrows), upload Jost 400 and 500 WOFF2 as the custom body font, as Coldbrook does with Barlow.
- Preload the Bodoni 400 WOFF2. That makes 4–5 font files in total.
- **Fallback if uploads are blocked:** Playfair Display + Jost, both from the library. It is less distinctive, but it holds the system.

## 7. Logo

**Wordmark.** "LUMORA" set in Bodoni Moda Medium caps (opsz 28), tracked +200 and optically kerned. **The O is redrawn** as a true circle: outer Ø = cap height + 2% overshoot. It keeps Didone stress, with sides at 13.4% of Ø and hairlines at 3.6%, so it reads as a letter rather than a foreign ring. A solid **glint** dot (Ø 15% of the O, Glint `#B8925A`) sits on the rim at **45° (1:30)**, where light would catch a glass edge. The glint is the only color in the logo.

**Monogram ("the Halo O").** The O and glint on their own. Used for the favicon, social avatar, the debossed jar lid, ritual pins, the loader and the focus ring. Below 24 px use the **small cut**: stress 18%, hairline 8%, glint 22%.

**Construction and usage**
- Clear space = 1 × O diameter on all sides.
- Minimum sizes: wordmark 80 px / 20 mm wide; monogram 16 px / 5 mm.
- Colorways: Umber on Porcelain (primary); Porcelain on Umber with a Champagne glint; Gilt screen-print on glass with the glint hot-foiled in champagne; Porcelain ink on the amber Sunroom bottle.
- Header lock-up: centered wordmark, 25 px cap height on desktop, 18 px on mobile.
- **Wordmark at sunset** (footer, desktop only): the wordmark at about 16vw, sinking below a horizon arc and lit from below by a Sunrise glow.
- Don'ts: no gradients or foil effects in digital use, no outline versions, no italic wordmark, never recolor the glint to any color except Glint or Champagne, never rotate the glint off 45°.
- Files to produce for the build: `lumora-wordmark.svg` (Umber and Porcelain), `lumora-halo-o.svg` (regular and small cut), favicon 32/180 px, a 1200 × 630 social share image.

## 8. Art direction

### 8.1 Packshots (Blender rig, every product, grid and PDP slot 1)
- **Backdrop:** seamless sweep color-locked to **Plaster `#EDE5DA`** (rig `backdrop: {type: "sweep", color: "#EDE5DA"}`; `match_backdrop` is on by default without sun). Tiles and renders merge with no visible box.
- **Light:** `soft_studio` preset, key light 52° left at about 5300 K, plus `kelvin_shift: -200` for warmth. The preset's backdrop light paints a **soft halo on the sweep behind each product at about 60% of its height**. That halo is our version of the references' tile treatment, and it makes frosted glass glow from behind. Raise its `E` to 8–9 for glass; leave the default for the opaque jar and stick.
- **Camera:** `pdp_4x5` (PDP), `packshot_1x1` (cards, cart, mega menu); 90–100 mm, 5–6° elevation, label square to camera, nothing cropped.
- **Ground contact:** a soft contact shadow plus the product's own **light pool**. The rig's glass shadow stand-in tints shadows by the liquid color: gold under Lightwell, white under Still Water, amber under Sunroom. No hard shadows in packshots.
- **Output:** 2048 px final (`--quality final`), AgX Medium High Contrast, the grade in 8.4, and alt text on every file ("Lightwell Serum, 30 ml frosted glass dropper with champagne collar").

### 8.3 Editorial photography (Unsplash brief)
- **Subjects:** bare skin with real texture (cheek, jaw, collarbone, shoulder) in one beam of window light or low sun; profiles with eyes closed or turned toward the light; hands holding or applying from frosted glass (hand crops can be composited with renders for PDP slot 3); a hands-only shot of a formulator at a bench with glassware.
- **Light:** warm, directional daylight (3500–4800 K): window beams, raking low sun, soft falloff into warm shadow. Hard light is welcome; flash is not.
- **Casting:** deep (Fitzpatrick V–VI), medium and light skin, ages about 25–55. Hands and shoulders appear as often as faces. Each of the six PDPs features a different person.
- **Crops:** tight and editorial. No direct-to-camera smiles and no full-body lifestyle shots. Leave quiet negative space for type.
- **Avoid:** studio flash and ring-light catchlights, heavy makeup, airbrushed skin (the claims rules apply), cool or blue grades, white clinical bathrooms, visible brands or text, prism or rainbow-filter clichés, lens-flare overload, AI-generated people.
- **Search terms:** "window light skin," "sunlight shoulder," "light on skin texture," "hand shadow sunlight," "woman eyes closed sunlight profile," "golden light hands." Keep a `photos/CREDITS.md` for every file.

### 8.4 The grade (one look for renders and photos)
- White balance +300 K warm, tint +3 magenta.
- Black point lifted to Umber `#2B231E` (no pure black); white point rolled to Porcelain `#F7F2EB` (no pure white).
- Gentle S-curve (+8 contrast). Saturation −15% globally, with skin hues (20–40°) and amber protected.
- Split tone: highlights Sunrise `#F1CFB4` at 10%, shadows Umber at 12%. Fine monochrome grain at 2%.
- Renders: AgX Medium High Contrast in Cycles, then the same LUT/curve (an exported `.cube`) applied in post to renders and Unsplash files alike.
- **Grading test:** place every image side by side at thumbnail size. Shadows, whites and skin must match.

### 8.5 Motifs, texture, transitions, icons
- **The halo:** a circle + glint (logo O, step rings, ritual pins, loader, focus ring).
- **The horizon arc:** the signature transition, a shallow convex arc (`viewBox 0 0 1440 64`, path `M0 64 V40 Q720 -24 1440 40 V64Z`) filled with the next section's color and traced by a 1 px Glint hairline at 50%. It appears **three times only**: a sunrise edge under the hero, at the top of the ritual band, and a sunset edge (inverted) from the closing band into the footer. On mobile it is 28 px tall.
- **The light pool:** the caustic a glass throws, as a soft elliptical Sunrise/gold radial gradient. Used on card hover, under pins, and in empty states ("Your bag is empty. Most people start with Lightwell.").
- **The ray:** 45° hairlines for diagrams, spec-row dividers and footnote links.
- **Texture:** only a 2.5% "frost grain" (a tileable 256 px monochrome noise PNG) on the hero and the dusk band. No paper or topo textures.
- **Icons:** a custom set on a 24 px grid with 1.25 px stroke and round caps, every icon drawn from circles and arcs. Umber on light, Champagne on dark. In feature contexts (promise strip, PDP benefits) each sits in a 46 px hairline ring with a 5 px Glint dot at 45°. Set: light-stable (bottle in rays), fragrance-free (slashed flower), % on the front, water-light (drop), free shipping (box), 30-day guarantee (circular arrow with check), sensitive-tested (hand), AM/PM (sun/moon halves).
- **Radius:** pills (999 px) for buttons, inputs, size pills and chips; **2 px** for cards, tiles and images; 50% for halo elements. Nothing else.
- **Motion:** one easing curve, `cubic-bezier(.22,.8,.26,1)`. Durations 200 ms (color), 420 ms (lift) and 1200 ms ("light" fades and glows; light takes its time). Image hover scales to 1.03 over 900 ms. Staggered fade-up reveals of 24 px at 80 ms. All of it is disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## 11. Signature details (what makes it feel custom-built)

1. **The Halo O system.** One mark generates the favicon, step rings, ritual pins, the loader (a glint orbiting the O once), the focus ring and the debossed jar lid.
2. **The light-disc hero with sun-drift.** A Cycles still plus one CSS radial-gradient overlay that slides 24 px over 12 s, like the sun moving. Static under reduced motion. It costs nothing in performance.
3. **The lit word.** One word per page-level headline is italic and sits in a soft Sunrise halo (an `em` with a blurred radial `::before`). The ink stays Umber, so contrast holds.
4. **Light captions.** Every still is labeled like a museum piece (product · time · kelvin · surface), set live in Bodoni italic + Jost caps.
5. **Horizon-arc transitions.** A shallow SVG arc with a Glint hairline: a sunrise edge under the hero, the ritual band, and a sunset edge into the footer. Three uses only.
6. **The day-arc page.** Section tones move from dawn (hero) through porcelain and plaster middays to the dusk band and the Umber footer. The homepage literally scrolls through one day.
7. **Cards that switch on.** On hover a Sunrise light pool fades in under the packshot (600 ms), the card lifts 4 px and the image scales to 1.03; the second image is a texture render. On mobile the pool shows at rest and the quick add stays visible.
8. **"Radiance is optics" diagram.** An SVG whose rays redraw from scattered to returned (stroke-dashoffset) as it enters the viewport.
9. **Halo pins.** The shoppable ritual uses Glint rings with Bodoni italic numerals and a slow glint pulse (2.8 s). The numbered list never disappears on mobile.
10. **Glint focus ring.** `:focus-visible` draws a 2 px Gilt ring with a 4 px Glint dot at 45°: accessibility as a brand detail.
11. **Sunrise shipping bar.** In the cart drawer the free-shipping bar fills from Plaster to Sunrise as a glint travels along it ("You're $12 from free shipping. Glint gets you there." then "Free shipping unlocked.").

## 12. Risks (and how we handle them)

1. **"Glow" is the most crowded idea in beauty.** It could read generic. *Mitigation:* lead with the optics thesis, clear-glass proof and light captions; ban "glow" adjectives in copy; let the disc-of-light imagery carry the mood.
2. **Low contrast is built into luminous palettes, and Didone hairlines vanish at small sizes.** *Mitigation:* AA-checked tokens (Gilt for small accent text, Umber only over Sunrise), Bodoni ≥ 24 px at opsz 28, Jost for all UI.
3. **Render feasibility.** The light disc and caustics rely on rig features: the disc needs a disk-shaped area light with a narrow spread (supported through job light overrides, not yet tested); true refractive caustics are noisy in Cycles. *Mitigation:* the rig's tinted glass-shadow stand-in, more samples for the hero only, and a disc/gobo fallback. Budget one test render before the mockup depends on S1.
4. **Clear-glass skepticism.** An expert may ask whether light degrades skincare. *Mitigation:* that is the point of the claim. Use only photostable actives (no vitamin C or retinoids), state the stability test in the trust row, put the cleanser in frosted PET for shower safety, and give the D3 amber honestly.
5. **Supplement complexity.** Sunroom brings food-supplement rules (EU-authorized claim wording, the US DSHEA disclaimer, upper limits, the K2/anticoagulant warning) and could dilute the skincare focus. *Mitigation:* frame it as a seasonal "06 · Within" step with plain warnings. If the client wants pure beauty, swap it for a body oil in the same cylinder pack with no other changes.
6. **Gradients can look cheap.** *Mitigation:* Sunrise appears in three moments only (hero light, ritual band, dusk band), always low-chroma, with no neon and no rainbow or prism effects.
7. **Didone plus gold can tip into generic "luxury".** *Mitigation:* sentence case, no gold text on light, no foil effects in the UI, generous white space, and a matte porcelain ground.
8. **Trademark clearance** is still needed for all six names, even after avoiding the known collisions (Halo Glow, Afterglow, Dawn, Lumene).
9. **Unsplash consistency.** Warm, directional skin photography with diverse casting exists, but quality varies. *Mitigation:* a strict crop (skin, hands, profiles), one LUT, and side-by-side grading tests; renders carry the product moments, so photos never need to show packaging.
10. **The day-arc background shift could read as inconsistency** if overdone. *Mitigation:* only the named tokens, never two adjacent bands in the same tone, and the dusk only at the end.
11. **Light captions must be true to the renders.** A wrong kelvin value is an easy catch for a detail-minded reviewer, so pull the values from the job files.
