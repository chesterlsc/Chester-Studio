Ingredients, explained

What’s in the glow.

Read every ingredient

Vitamin C 10%

Brightens the look of dull skin. A stable form, kept potent by amber glass.

Sea buckthorn oil 2%

Carotenoid-rich, and the reason Long Light is apricot. The color is the ingredient.

Apricot kernel oil

Soft slip that sinks in fast: 18% in Sunmelt, 40% in Gloaming.

Ceramides + squalane 5%

Help replenish the moisture barrier, so dry‑feeling skin feels comfortable again.

No mica, glitter or self-tan anywhere in the line, and no tint in the glow formulas. The glow should be your skin’s, not a coating.
