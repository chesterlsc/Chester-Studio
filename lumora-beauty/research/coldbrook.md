# Reference teardown: coldbrookoutfitters.com

> Studio research for **Lumora Beauty**. Goal: understand *why* this Minimog store feels polished so we can
> hit the same bar with our own brand, never copy it. Captured 2026-11-23 at 1440×900 (desktop) and
> 390×844 (mobile, 2x). Hero product studied: **Instant Pop Up Camping Tent**
> (`/products/automatic-tent-outdoor-camping-barbecue`), #1 in Best Sellers and pin #1 in the homepage kit.
>
> Screenshots stay in the session scratchpad (third-party images are never copied into this repo); paths are in the appendix.

---

## 0. TL;DR: where the polish comes from

1. **A coded design-system layer on top of stock Minimog.** Two CSS-only "skin" sections (`sp_chrome_skin`
   site-wide, `sp_index_skin` homepage) define tokens (`--sp-paper`, `--sp-rust`, `--sp-ease`…) and restyle
   *every* stock component (buttons, cards, nav, footer, cart drawer, tabs, testimonials) so stock and custom
   sections read as one system. Comments name the kit "Stone Pine Co.".
2. **Five bespoke sections where it matters most**: hero, activity grid, shoppable kit scene, brand promise,
   closing "forest fade". Everything else is stock Minimog (product tabs, featured collection, image cards,
   blog, slideshow, testimonials, footer).
3. **A strict, small palette**: paper / deeper paper / card cream / ink + one rust accent (two shades). Mapped
   into Minimog colour schemes so stock sections inherit it.
4. **One display face doing all the "voice" work**: Barlow Condensed 600, uppercase, tracked, from the 13px
   announcement bar to the 92px H1. Source Sans Pro for reading text.
5. **Signature transitions instead of hard section cuts**: torn mountain-ridge edge under the hero, torn
   paper edges on the video band, a tree-line that fades paper into the ink footer, a topographic texture.
6. **Art direction consistency**: AI-generated, product-referenced lifestyle scenes in one environment (misty
   PNW lakes, conifers, granite) and one light (overcast, cool, desaturated), with wardrobe matched to the rust accent.
7. **Card discipline**: every product card is the same boxed cream panel, 1:1 tile, multiply-blended packshot,
   2-line clamped title, price pinned to the bottom, equal heights per row.
8. **Micro-interaction layer**: one easing curve, two durations, 1px button lift, 3px card lift, 1.04 image
   zoom, underline wipes, pulsing hotspot pins, staggered fade-up reveals, all switched off for reduced motion.
9. **The PDP is the weak spot.** It is near-stock Minimog: dropdown variants, an outlined Add to cart that
   loses to the purple Shop Pay button, no trust row, no bundle, no FAQ. This is where Lumora can clearly beat it.

- **Scale**: 13 · 15 · 16 · 17 · 18 · 20 · 24.8 · 33 · 46 · 92. H1 is exactly 2× H2. Theme vars: `--font-h1-desktop: 64` (unused by the custom hero), `--font-h2-desktop: 46`, `--font-h3-desktop: 32`, `--font-h4-desktop: 22`, base 16, line-height base 26.
- **Rule of thumb**: condensed caps for anything a shopper *scans* (labels, headings, buttons, prices); humanist sans for anything they *read*.
- **Font loading**: Barlow Condensed is a **custom upload**: four `@font-face` rules with family `M-Heading-Font` pointing at
  `https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0997/4851/2063/files/BarlowCondensed-{Regular,Medium,SemiBold,Bold}.ttf`
  (Minimog's custom-font setting, hence the alias). The CDN negotiates WOFF2 (SemiBold = 39 KB), but it is not preloaded.
  Source Sans Pro comes from the **Shopify font library** (`/cdn/fonts/source_sans_pro/…woff2`, 400/700 + italics, preloaded).

### 4.3 Spacing, grid, rhythm

- **Containers**: `container-fluid` = max 1 520px content + 60px gutters (`--fluid-container-offset`), i.e. 1 320px content at 1440. Mobile gutter **15px**. The PDP uses the narrower `container` (1 200px, 1 170px content), so its edges (x=135) do not line up with the header logo (x=60).
- **Grids** (1440): activity 6-up, 14px gap, 4:5; product rail 6-up, 16px gap, 207px cards; featured 5-up; stories 3-up, 15px gap, 4:3; blog 3-up, 30px gap, 16:9; kit split 32% / 68% with 56px gap; promise 30% / 70% with 60px gap.
- **Section padding**: settings from 40 to 96px, scaled ×0.8 by Minimog at 1440 (e.g. 80→64, 96→77). Paper sections get ~32–45px per side (≈80px between two paper sections); dark/feature bands get 64–77px. **Mobile: a uniform 40/40.**
- **Inside sections**: section header → content 28px (20px with tabs); eyebrow → heading 8–14px; heading → text 14px; paragraph measure 40–56ch.

### 4.4 Radius, borders, shadows, motion

- **Radius**: one 2px token (`--sp-radius`, `--btn-border-radius`, `--inputs-radius`) for buttons, inputs, cards, tiles and images. Exceptions: native selects 4px (stock, never overridden), pins/avatars 50%.
- **Borders**: 1px `#D6CAB5` on cards; hairlines at 12–18% of foreground on dark; tile inset `0 0 0 1px rgba(27,30,26,.06)`.
- **Shadows** (hover only): card `0 14px 30px rgba(27,30,26,.08)`; primary button `0 8px 22px rgba(27,30,26,.18)`; popover `0 16px 36px rgba(27,30,26,.18)`.
- **Motion**: `--sp-ease: cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1)`, `--sp-fast: 180ms`, `--sp-slow: 380ms`; image zoom 1.04–1.045 over .8–.9s `cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1)`; Minimog fade-in-up 600ms with a cascade stagger; `prefers-reduced-motion` removes lifts, zooms, pulses and wipes.

### 4.5 Buttons and links

- **Primary**: filled rust (`#9E4520` on light, `#B5532A` on dark), cream text, 46px tall, padding 10×30 (10×20 mobile), Barlow 16 / 600 / .1em caps, 2px radius; hover = 1px lift + shadow + darker rust; optional arrow that nudges 4px. Full width on mobile hero and closing CTA.
- **Secondary**: transparent, 1px ink (light) or cream (dark) border, same metrics.
- **Text link**: Barlow 15px caps .1em in rust, 4px padding-bottom with a 1px underline at 35% opacity that turns solid on hover; arrow nudges 3px.
- **Quick view**: white button that turns rust on card hover. Mobile: 36px round bag icon at the bottom-centre of the image.

## 6. Imagery

- **Lifestyle = AI-generated, product-referenced scenes.** Tells: the folding stove tile shows a garbled "Tı artisan Titanium" mark; supplier brand marks (e.g. "BLACK DOG" on the chair) survive in scenes; the hero backpack is the catalogue's 75L pack. The same products (tent, chair, stove, mat, lantern) recur across hero, tiles, kit scene, mega menu and journal.
- **How consistency is achieved**: one environment (alpine lakes, conifers, granite), one weather and light (overcast, fog, soft and low-contrast, cool desaturated greens and greys), people shown from behind or as hands only (no faces to get wrong, POV is aspirational), and **wardrobe matched to the accent** (rust jacket, orange trim). Framing is normalised by the sections themselves: one ratio per section (4:5 tiles, 4:3 stories and scene, 16:9 journal) and identical bottom scrims.
- **Packshots = supplier images** (800×800, white or grey backgrounds, mixed angles and lighting, some with accessories laid out). They are normalised in CSS: cream tile, `object-fit: contain`, 6% padding, `mix-blend-mode: multiply` to drop the white. On the PDP, a generated selector list blends only the white-background files. It mostly works, but baked-in frames and grey edges still show (visible on the tent's main image).
- **Off-grade exceptions**: the video band (sunny coastal stock footage, warm, faces to camera) and PDP lifestyle shots from the supplier (e.g. a woman playing guitar in the tent).
- **Graphic assets**: Tabler-style line icons (stroke 1.3–1.8, inline SVG): truck, arrows-exchange, shield-check, mountain, tent, trekking, backpack, campfire, caravan, first-aid kit, tools, leaf, award; topographic contour SVG; torn-edge SVGs; tree-line PNG.

## 11. Copy tone and structure

- **Voice**: plain, practical, quietly confident; "spec-led" positioning repeated everywhere (announcement, values, journal, reviews). No contractions ("does not", "It is almost never the bag") and British spelling ("catalogue") on a US-shipping store: credible but a little stiff.
- **Headline formula**: 2–4 words, title case in source, rendered uppercase: verb or noun + outcome ("Built For The Wild", "Set Up Camp Properly", "Gear You Can Count On", "Ready For Anything", "The Wild Is Waiting"). One idiom family (trail, pack, camp, wild).
- **Hero stack**: H1 (4 words) → accent tagline (4 words with a full stop) → 2 sentences built on **"X, not Y"** ("picked for real conditions, not for the catalogue") + a **rule-of-three** ("tents that pitch in minutes, packs that carry weight properly, and light, warmth and hot food…") → 2 CTAs → trust row (caps title + one-line clarifier: "30-Day Returns / Unused and boxed, no fuss").
- **Eyebrows** mark *story* sections only (kit, promise, closing CTA, story cards); product rails get bare headings. Eyebrows state the angle or occasion: "Buy By The Trip, Not By The Item", "Why Coldbrook", "Ready When You Are", "Warm On Any Ridge", "Basecamp After Dark".
- **Value columns**: 2–3 word caps title + one sentence of ≤10 words with a contrast ("Picked for weather and terrain, not for the photo." / "Higher-ticket problem solvers. No filler, no clutter.").
- **CTAs are specific verbs in the brand idiom**: "Shop the range", "Shop the kit", "Start kitting out", "Read our story", "Browse everything". The generic "Shop now" survives only on story cards.
- **Microcopy**: instructional ("Tap a number to see a piece on its own, or take the whole set in one go."), risk reversal next to the final CTA ("Returns stay open for 30 days if something turns out not to be right."), a named newsletter with an anti-spam promise ("Field Notes… No noise.").
- **Reviews and testimonials**: caps headline that summarises the benefit ("Pitched In The Rain, Stayed Dry") + 2 sentences + first name and initial.
- **PDP copy structure**: bold one-line benefit → sensory paragraph → Key Features (feature → benefit) → Use Cases → Specs → Size chart. Good content, poor presentation (one flat column).

## 13. Polish checklist for Lumora (testable)

1. **One section-header spec everywhere, PDP included**: optional eyebrow (13px, 600, .16em, caps, accent) → H2 (46px desktop / 32px mobile, single line-height ≈1.05, single tracking value) → optional right-aligned text link (drops under the heading on mobile). Check: computed H2 line-height and letter-spacing are identical in every section.
2. **Eyebrows only on story sections**; product rails get a bare heading. Every eyebrow names a benefit, ritual or occasion, never a generic label.
3. **Every product card**: same media ratio, same tile background, same image padding; title clamped to 2 lines with reserved height; price pinned to the bottom; **equal card heights in every row on every template** (home rails and PDP recommendations).
4. **One radius token** for buttons, inputs, selects, cards, tiles, images and popovers. Check: no computed `border-radius` outside {token, 50%}.
5. **Buttons**: one height (46px), one tracking, one primary colour per background; hover = 1px lift + soft shadow; arrow icons nudge 3–4px. Text contrast ≥4.5:1 on both light and dark variants.
6. **Exactly one "view all" pattern** (right-aligned header link with a 1px underline that goes solid on hover).
7. **A signature transition motif** of our own, used 2–3 times at the same kind of moment (e.g. under the hero, into the footer), built as SVG/CSS on stock sections. No hard white-to-dark cuts elsewhere.
8. **Background rhythm**: alternate base / deeper tint / dark bands; never two identical dark bands adjacent; no pure-white page background; brand texture on at most 2 sections at ≤8% opacity.
9. **Motion system**: one easing curve, two durations (~180ms / ~380ms), image hover zoom ≈1.04 over ~0.8s, staggered fade-up reveals; everything disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
10. **Imagery grading test**: lay every lifestyle image side by side; same light, white balance and contrast; props or wardrobe echo the accent colour. Zero off-grade stock.
11. **Packshots**: all six products rendered on the same background, camera family, light and shadow; source ≥2 048px; descriptive alt text on every image.
12. **Hero (desktop)**: H1 + tagline + 2-sentence body + 2 CTAs + 3-item trust row (icon, caps title, one-line clarifier) separated by hairlines; scrim keeps the H1 ≥4.5:1 over the photo; hero fills the first 900px screen.
13. **Hero (mobile)**: dedicated 4:5 (or shorter) art; H1 **and** primary CTA visible inside the first 390×844 screen; announcement fits one line.
14. **Shoppable routine/scene**: numbered pins in the accent with a light ring and gentle pulse; popover with thumbnail, name and arrow; the numbered list stays visible on mobile; popovers never cause horizontal overflow.
15. **Header**: tracked caps nav with a wipe-in underline; sticky on scroll-up; at least one mega menu with imagery; announcement in the display face.
16. **Footer**: logo + one-line brand promise, 3 link columns, a *named* newsletter with a one-line value promise, payment icons, accordions on mobile; the section above flows into it.
17. **Contrast**: every text/background pair ≥4.5:1 (≥3:1 only for ≥24px text). Run a checker on eyebrows, links, buttons and text over images.
18. **PDP above the fold** (1440×900): title, rating with count, price, 2–4 benefit bullets with icons, variant pills, quantity + ATC, and a trust/delivery microcopy line are all visible without scrolling.
19. **PDP CTA hierarchy**: the brand-filled ATC is the most prominent element in the buy box; the dynamic checkout button sits below it and is visually quieter; quantity and ATC share one height.
20. **PDP depth below the fold**: content in tabs or accordions (ingredients, how to use, results, FAQ); bundle/routine upsell; reviews restyled to our tokens with the rating summary visible; bullet lists at line-height ≤1.6.
21. **Sticky ATC** on desktop (after scroll) and mobile, styled in our tokens; never stacked awkwardly with the bottom nav or back-to-top.
22. **Grid alignment**: the PDP uses the same container and outer gutters as the header and homepage (60px desktop, 15–20px mobile). Check: left edge of the PDP content equals the logo's left edge.
23. **No horizontal overflow** at 390, 768 and 1440: `document.documentElement.scrollWidth === clientWidth` with `overflow-x: clip` removed.
24. **Copy**: one voice, US spelling, headlines of 2–4 words, every CTA a specific verb phrase (no bare "Shop now"), risk-reversal microcopy next to the main CTA clusters, cosmetics-compliant claims, placeholder stats footnoted.
25. **Performance hygiene**: hero image ≤200 KB at 1920w; no autoplaying video over ~3 MB (none on mobile); heading font WOFF2 preloaded; no dead CSS in the skin.
