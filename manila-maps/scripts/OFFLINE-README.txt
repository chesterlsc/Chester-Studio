MANILA MAPS — OFFLINE VERSION
=============================

Works with no internet. Keep the two files in this folder together:
  • Manila Maps.html   – the app
  • manila.mmap        – the map of Metro Manila (streets, places, 3D buildings, routing)

How to open
1. Unzip this folder into your phone's Files (e.g. Download or Documents).
2. Open "Manila Maps.html" with Chrome (or any modern browser).
3. The first time, tap "Open map file" and choose manila.mmap.
   The browser keeps a copy so next time it opens straight to the map.

Tip: the Android app (ManilaMaps-*.apk) is the best experience — GPS follow,
voice directions and the map stored permanently in the app.
