<Callout type="caution" title="Dirty water kills, too.">
  Poor water, toilets and hygiene together kill about 1.4 million people a year (WHO).
</Callout>

<Scenario when="Day 1 of a long blackout" move="Water first. Fill clean, covered containers, then sort the food.">
  The power went out this morning. Your family wants to save the freezer food first.

  You stop and think. Most adults can safely eat less for a while. Nobody can go without water. Most people die in 3 to 7 days without it.

  So you start with water. You fill every clean jug that has a lid. Then you turn to the freezer.
</Scenario>
