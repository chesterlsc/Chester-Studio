<Callout type="tip" title="Long term, plan for more.">
  WHO: 7.5 L (2 gal) a day is the bare minimum for most people. In an emergency, WHO sets at least 15 L (4 gal). About 20 L (5.3 gal) covers basic hygiene.
</Callout>

<Scenario when="Before storm season" move="Store at least 3 days now, then keep adding until you reach 2 weeks.">
  You live with your partner and your 8-year-old. You want water ready before storm season.

  You do the math: 3 people × 3 days × 1 gallon. That is 9 gallons (34 L).

  You fill and cap 9 clean gallon jugs. That is the minimum. Each payday, you add a few more. You are working toward 2 weeks.
</Scenario>
