<Callout type="danger" title="Get help now if someone:">
  Is confused, faints, or has no pee. Has a fast heartbeat or fast breathing.
  Has blue, grey, pale or blotchy skin or lips, or cold skin, or is hard to wake. Has a fever over 102°F (39°C).
  Or gets worse even after drinking.
</Callout>

<Callout type="caution" title="Get help the same day if:">
  A baby's soft spot sinks in, or a baby has few tears or fewer wet diapers. A child seems drowsy.
  You feel dizzy on standing, and it does not go away.
</Callout>
