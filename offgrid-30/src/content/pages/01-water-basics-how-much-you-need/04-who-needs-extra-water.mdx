<Callout type="caution" title="Too much plain water, fast, can harm.">
  Drinking far too much can cause low blood salt (hyponatremia). Drink often. Check pee color. Don't force huge amounts.
</Callout>

<Scenario when="Week 2, a heat wave" move="Hard work in heat: 1 cup (237 mL) every 15–20 minutes, starting before thirst.">
  It is mid-afternoon and very hot. Your teen is splitting wood in the yard. They haven't had a drink since lunch. They say they aren't thirsty.

  You call a break in the shade. You hand them a full cup of water and set a timer.

  Every 15 minutes, they stop and drink another cup.
</Scenario>
