<Callout type="tip" title="Example: a 20 kg (44 lb) dog on dry food">
  20 × 63–73 mL = about 1.3–1.5 L (0.3–0.4 gal) a day. For 2 weeks, store about 18–20 L (5 gal).
</Callout>

<Scenario when="Summer, day 5 without power" move="Count every animal in your water plan. Give milking animals and hot animals more.">
  Your well pump runs on power, so it is silent. You have 2 milking goats and a dog.

  In this heat, a milking goat can drink about 15 L (4 gal) a day. The pair needs about 30 L (8 gal). The dog needs more on top.

  You fill their troughs from your stored barrels. Then you start the generator to run the well.
</Scenario>
