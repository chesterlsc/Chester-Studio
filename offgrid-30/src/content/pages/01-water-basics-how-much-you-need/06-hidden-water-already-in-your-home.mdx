<Callout type="caution" title="In doubt? Treat it.">
  Treat any water you are unsure of before drinking. This matters most if a water line broke before you shut the main valve.
</Callout>

<Scenario when="Day 2, a water main breaks" move="Use heater, pipe and ice water. Never use a tank with cleaner, the bowl or radiators.">
  The radio says a water main broke in town. You shut your main valve. Your stored jugs are low.

  You turn off power to the water heater. You close its intake valve and open a hot tap upstairs. Then you fill clean jugs from the drain at the bottom.

  Your kids point to the toilet tank. It has a blue cleaner tablet in it. You say no.
</Scenario>
