<Callout type="tip" title="Before a freeze: outdoor taps.">
  Close the inside valve to each outdoor tap. Open the outdoor tap so it drains. Leave it open, so leftover water can expand without breaking the pipe.
</Callout>

<Scenario when="The night of a big storm" move="Close the house valve now. Keep it closed until officials say the water is safe.">
  The radio says sewer and water lines broke across town. Your taps still run.

  You go to the basement, to the valve with the orange tag. Last spring you tested it, so you know it turns. You close it all the way.

  Now dirty water can't flow in. The water in your heater and toilet tanks stays in your house.
</Scenario>
