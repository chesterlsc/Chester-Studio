<Callout type="tip" title="No calculator? Do it by hand.">
  People × days × 1 gallon = gallons. Times 3.8 = liters.
  Example: 4 people × 14 days = 56 gallons (212 L). That is 12 jugs of 5 gal (19 L). Using drums only? You need 2 drums of 55 gal (208 L).
</Callout>
