✅ How the facts were checked

This book contains facts only: what official guidance says, what studies measured and what happened in real events. There are no theories and no predictions.

1 · Researched

Each chapter was researched from official and scientific sources: WHO, CDC, FDA, USDA, EPA, FEMA, NOAA, the American Heart Association, Red Cross, Cochrane reviews and peer-reviewed journals, using the latest guidance available in 2026.

2 · Fact-checked

A separate reviewer then re-checked every dose, temperature, time and number against sources, and removed anything that could not be verified.

3 · Cited

Every claim links to its source: 648 citations to 213 sources. Tap a small number to see the source.

4 · Honest about how it was made

The research, writing and checking were done with AI assistance (Claude by Anthropic), with each fact tied to a public source so you can verify it yourself.

⚠️ This is general information, not a substitute for professional medical care, hands-on first-aid and CPR training, or local emergency services. Take a first-aid course now while trainers still exist. Guidance changes: the date on every source tells you how current it is.

📖 Full contents

  • 🔴 Critical: Can save a life in minutes to days. Learn first.
  • 🟠 High: Needed in the first weeks.
