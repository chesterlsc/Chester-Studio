Shelter, Heat & Light

Staying warm, cool, dry and lit without electricity, and doing it without poisoning yourself with carbon monoxide or burning the house down.

🏠Chapter 104·🟠 High·⏱ 13 min
Preparing Your Home for a Long Outage
Choose one warm, safe room now. Few windows, two ways out, and space for everyone, pets included.12
💨Chapter 106·🔴 Critical·⏱ 13 min
Carbon Monoxide: The Blackout Killer
Never run a generator, grill, camp stove or car engine inside a home, garage or carport. Open doors and windows do not make it safe.146
