🔥 Boiling: the most reliable method

Bring water to a rolling boil and keep it there for 1 minute. Above 2,000 m (6,500 ft), boil it for 3 minutes.1

Let it cool, then pour it into clean containers with tight lids.1 See Storing Water.

  1. Settle and filter cloudy water

    Let it stand, then pour it through a clean cloth or coffee filter.

  2. Bring to a rolling boil

    Big bubbles that do not stop when you stir.

  3. Keep boiling for 1 minute

    3 minutes above 2,000 m (6,500 ft).1

  4. Cool and store covered

    Use clean containers with tight lids.

Boiling does not remove chemicals

If the water may contain fuel or toxic chemicals, boiling will not make it safe. Use a different source.1

More bleach is always safer.

The EPA doses already work. A 2014 study found the recommended doses were higher than needed to kill the pathogens tested.4

Do

  • Use unscented bleach
  • Mix and wait 30 minutes

Don't

  • Use scented or "splash-less" bleach
  • Drink before the wait is over

🧠 Remember this

How long do you boil water?

1 minute at a rolling boil. 3 minutes above 2,000 m (6,500 ft).

How much 6% bleach per gallon?

8 drops, then wait 30 minutes.

Does boiling remove fuel?

No. Find another source.

Cloudy water?

Double the bleach, or filter it first.
