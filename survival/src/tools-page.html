• Water: store at least 3.8 liters (1 gallon) per person per day, for at least 3 days and ideally 2 weeks.1
  • Bleach: 8 drops of 6% (or 6 drops of 8.25%) plain unscented bleach per gallon of clear water, then wait 30 minutes.2
  • Food: humanitarian planners use 2,100 kcal per person per day as the starting figure.4
  • ORS: 6 level teaspoons of sugar + ½ level teaspoon of salt in 1 liter of clean water.6

💧 Water

Boiling is the most reliable way to kill germs: keep water at a rolling boil for 1 minute, or 3 minutes above 2,000 m (6,500 ft).3 Boiling and bleach do not remove fuel or chemicals.3 Read Disinfecting Water.

🌾 Food

The 2,100 kcal figure is a planning average for a whole population. Real needs vary by age, size, pregnancy and workload, so adjust it.45 Read Calories & Nutrition.

🩺 Medical

Adult CPR: push hard and fast in the center of the chest, at 100 to 120 compressions per minute and at least 5 cm (2 inches) deep, letting the chest come fully back up.78 Read CPR & Choking and Dehydration & ORS.

🧼 Hygiene

Scrub all surfaces of your hands with soap for at least 20 seconds, then rinse and dry.9 Read Handwashing & Hygiene.
