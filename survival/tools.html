🧮 Calculators & timers

These work offline. Every number they use comes from the sources listed under each tool.

  • Water: store at least 3.8 liters (1 gallon) per person per day, for at least 3 days and ideally 2 weeks.1
  • Bleach: 8 drops of 6% (or 6 drops of 8.25%) plain unscented bleach per gallon of clear water, then wait 30 minutes.2
  • Food: humanitarian planners use 2,100 kcal per person per day as the starting figure.4
  • ORS: 6 level teaspoons of sugar + ½ level teaspoon of salt in 1 liter of clean water.6

💧 Water

Boiling is the most reliable way to kill germs: keep water at a rolling boil for 1 minute, or 3 minutes above 2,000 m (6,500 ft).3 Boiling and bleach do not remove fuel or chemicals.3 Read Disinfecting Water.

🌾 Food

The 2,100 kcal figure is a planning average for a whole population. Real needs vary by age, size, pregnancy and workload, so adjust it.45 Read Calories & Nutrition.

🩺 Medical

Adult CPR: push hard and fast in the center of the chest, at 100 to 120 compressions per minute and at least 5 cm (2 inches) deep, letting the chest come fully back up.78 Read CPR & Choking and Dehydration & ORS.

🧼 Hygiene

Scrub all surfaces of your hands with soap for at least 20 seconds, then rinse and dry.9 Read Handwashing & Hygiene.

📚 Sources

  1. CDC: How to Create an Emergency Water Supply (2024). At least 1 gallon per person per day for 3 days; a 2-week supply if possible; more for hot climates, pregnancy and illness.
  2. EPA: Emergency Disinfection of Drinking Water (2024). Bleach drops per gallon by strength; double for cloudy, colored or very cold water.
  3. CDC: Boil Water Advisory (2024). Boil 1 minute (3 minutes above 6,500 ft); 30-minute bleach wait; boiling does not remove chemicals.
  4. Global Food Security Cluster Handbook: Food Assistance (2023). 2,100 kcal per person per day planning figure (Sphere standard).
  5. National Academies: High-Energy, Nutrient-Dense Emergency Relief Food Product (2002). 2,100 kcal per day used for emergency food planning.
  6. WHO Regional Office for the Eastern Mediterranean: Cholera guidance (n.d.). Home ORS: half a teaspoon of salt and six teaspoons of sugar in one liter of clean water.
  7. Vanderbilt University Medical Center: Heartsaver CPR AED summary, 2025 AHA Guidelines (2025). Adult compressions 100–120 per minute, at least 2 inches deep.
  8. Resuscitation Council UK: 2025 Guidelines (2025). Compression rate 100–120 per minute, depth 5–6 cm.
  9. CDC: When and how to clean hands (poster) (2023). Scrub all hand surfaces with soap; when to wash.
  10. USDA FoodData Central (2025). Energy per 100 g of dry rice, beans, oats and oil used in the food calculator.
